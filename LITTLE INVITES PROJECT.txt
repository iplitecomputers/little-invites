## Business Concept

Little Invites is a luxury digital invitation creation service.

The business allows customers to order personalized electronic invitations for events such as:

- Weddings
- Birthdays
- Anniversaries
- Engagements
- Baby showers
- Baptisms
- Graduations
- Corporate events
- Other special occasions

Instead of sending traditional invitation cards, customers receive a beautiful interactive electronic invitation website with a unique shareable link.

The invitation link can be sent through:

- Facebook Messenger
- WhatsApp
- SMS
- Email
- Other messaging platforms

When a guest opens the link on their phone, they experience an animated digital envelope that opens to reveal their personalized invitation.

The objective is to create an elegant, memorable, premium experience.

# 6. IMPORTANT: ENVELOPE ANIMATION REQUIREMENTS

This is an important visual feature of Little Invites.

I want a realistic, elegant envelope-opening experience.

The intended animation:

1. The guest opens the invitation page.
2. They see a closed envelope with a wax seal.
3. They click the envelope button or wax seal.
4. The wax seal disappears.
5. The envelope flap opens.
6. The invitation letter emerges from INSIDE the envelope.
7. The letter slides upward smoothly.
8. The envelope remains behind the emerging letter.
9. The complete invitation details become visible.

VERY IMPORTANT:

The letter must NOT suddenly appear on top of the envelope.

It must look as if the letter is physically being pulled out of the envelope.

The envelope's front pocket should initially cover the lower part of the letter.

The letter should move upward from behind the envelope front.

The animation must have correct layering and stacking order.

The opening animation must work on desktop and mobile.

The final presentation should be smooth, realistic, and premium.

Do not sacrifice the physical envelope effect just to make the letter visible.
